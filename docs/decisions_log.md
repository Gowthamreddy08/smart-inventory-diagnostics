## 1. Architectural & Methodological Decisions

| Decision ID | Date | Decision Area | Decision | Rationale | Alternatives Considered | Impact on Analysis | Status | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DEC-001** | 2026-10-28 | **Analysis Grain** | Establish primary grain at `SKU × Store × Date`, supplier grain at `SKU × Supplier × PO`, and segmentation grain at `SKU × Store`. | Store-level customer demand patterns and supplier fulfillment lag vary dramatically by geography; aggregating to company-SKU masks localized stockout risks. | • Company × SKU × Date<br>• Store × Category × Week | Preserves granularity needed for local store replenishment; requires robust SQL window performance. | **Approved** | Senior Data Analyst |
| **DEC-002** | 2026-10-28 | **ABC Classification Basis** | Use annual cumulative **Contribution Margin** ($\text{Revenue} - \text{COGS}$) as the primary ABC Pareto sorting measure. | High-revenue products with near-zero gross margin tie up disproportionate working capital without driving corporate profitability. Prioritizes items delivering true bottom-line cash flow. | • Gross Sales Revenue<br>• Total Unit Volume | Prioritizes working capital protection for high-profit lines ($AX$ cash cows). | **Approved** | Inventory Planning Lead |
| **DEC-003** | 2026-10-28 | **XYZ Calculation Window** | Calculate Coefficient of Variation ($CV$) over **365 days of in-stock operating history** with a minimum sample size of 30 in-stock days. | Full-year window captures seasonal variance and holiday spikes, preventing short-term volatility distortions. | • Rolling 30-day window<br>• Rolling 90-day window | Requires 1 full year of historical data; new SKUs with $< 30$ days history inherit category defaults. | **Approved** | Lead Supply Chain Analyst |
| **DEC-004** | 2026-10-28 | **XYZ CV Thresholds** | Adopt initial thresholds: $X \le 0.5$, $0.5 < Y \le 1.0$, $Z > 1.0$, validated against empirical demand distributions. | Standard industry cutoffs provide clear operational tiers. Validation against the actual dataset's empirical demand distribution prevents artificial skewing. | • Percentile-based cutoffs (e.g., 33rd/66th)<br>• $X \le 0.3$, $0.3 < Y \le 0.8$, $Z > 0.8$ | Initial thresholds confirmed: median CV is 0.616, min 0.296, max 1.048; captures natural demand clusters. | **Approved** | Senior Data Analyst |
| **DEC-005** | 2026-10-28 | **Stockout Definition** | Define stockout as $\text{Stock-on-Hand} \le 0$ on an **active store trading day**. | Distinguishes between planned store closures/holidays and actual stock depletion during customer trading hours. | • Daily Sales $= 0$<br>• $\text{SOH} < \text{Safety Stock}$ | Accurately identifies shelf depletion without counting scheduled store closures as stockout days. | **Approved** | Store Operations Lead |
| **DEC-006** | 2026-10-28 | **Lost-Sales Estimation Method** | Estimate unconstrained demand during stockouts using the **rolling 4-week same-weekday average over in-stock trading days**. | Captures intra-week shopping patterns (e.g., Friday/Saturday volume surges) without complex black-box econometric modeling. | • Prior 30-day simple moving average<br>• Machine learning regression (XGBoost) | Provides an intuitive, explainable counterfactual baseline; assumes no customer brand substitution. | **Approved** | Business Intelligence Lead |
| **DEC-007** | 2026-10-28 | **Service Levels to Test** | Test three Cycle Service Levels in policy backtesting: **$90\%$ ($Z=1.282$)**, **$95\%$ ($Z=1.645$)**, and **$99\%$ ($Z=2.326$)**. | Spans the spectrum from cost-conscious replenishment ($90\%$) to high-availability premium retail ($99\%$). | • Single static $95\%$ level<br>• Unit fill rate targets | Generates an explicit trade-off curve between holding cost and stockout protection for leadership. | **Approved** | VP of Supply Chain |
| **DEC-008** | 2026-10-28 | **Holding-Cost Rate Assumption** | Apply an industry-standard annual carrying cost rate of **$22\%$ of unit cost** ($0.0603\%$ daily). | Composed of: $10\%$ cost of capital, $4\%$ storage/warehousing, $2\%$ insurance/taxes, and $6\%$ obsolescence/shrinkage. | • Flat $15\%$ rate<br>• SKU-specific storage rate | Grounded in standard supply chain literature; provides a realistic balance-sheet penalty for excess stock. | **Approved** | Finance Controller |
| **DEC-009** | 2026-10-28 | **Replenishment Policy Architecture** | Implement **Continuous-Review ROP** as the primary operational engine and **Periodic-Review Order-Up-To** ($R=7$) as an evaluative alternative. | Enables real-time procurement alerts while supporting retail stores that operate on fixed weekly vendor delivery days. | • Continuous ROP only<br>• Periodic Order-Up-To only | Dashboard and simulation support both continuous and periodic policies to reflect real vendor constraints. | **Approved** | Procurement Operations |
| **DEC-010** | 2026-10-28 | **Excess-Inventory Definition** | Primary definition: $\text{Excess Units} = \max(0, \text{SOH} - \text{Order-Up-To Level})$. Secondary diagnostic flag: stock exceeding $60$ days of forward demand ($\text{DOH} > 60$). | Aligns primary excess directly to mathematical replenishment ceilings while preserving a standard operational slow-moving inventory metric. | • Stock $> 2 \times \text{ROP}$<br>• Single definition only | Eliminates ambiguity between operational order ceiling breaches and long-term slow-moving stock flags. | **Approved** | Inventory Planning Lead |
| **DEC-011** | 2026-10-28 | **Spoilage Module Treatment** | Treat spoilage as an **isolated synthetic extension module**, clearly labeled as simulated and completely excluded from core stockout/replenishment KPIs. | Public retail transaction datasets lack real expiration and waste write-off logs; prevents misrepresenting synthetic data as observed truth. | • Merge spoilage into holding cost<br>• Exclude spoilage entirely | Maintains professional portfolio integrity; core stockout and replenishment engine remains 100% empirical. | **Approved** | Senior Data Analyst Mentor |
| **DEC-012** | 2026-10-28 | **Data Generation & Ingestion Approach** | Ingest/generate a controlled synthetic enterprise retail dataset with realistic distributions, seasonal spikes, supplier delivery lags, and deliberate data anomalies for audit. | Guarantees known ground-truth parameters to validate data quality assertions, ROP calibration, and policy backtesting. | • Public Kaggle Walmart dataset<br>• Public Kaggle DataCo dataset | Ensures 100% control over schema constraints, edge cases, and known injected anomalies to prove audit capabilities. | **Approved** | Senior Data Analyst |

| **DEC-013** | 2026-10-29 | **Raw Immutability Rule** | Raw ingested CSVs in `data/raw/` must remain bit-for-bit immutable. All anomaly isolation occurs through dedicated quarantine files in `data/quarantine/`. | Prevents destructive modification of source truth and ensures auditability. | • In-place CSV correction<br>• Delete anomalous rows | Proven by automated SHA-256 hash assertions in test suite. | **Approved** | Data Quality Lead |
| **DEC-014** | 2026-10-29 | **Negative Inventory Rule** | Quarantine negative inventory records into `quarantined_inventory_snapshots.csv`; exclude from inventory position calculations rather than forward-filling. | Forward-filling negative values creates phantom inventory and biases turnover calculations. | • Forward-fill prior positive SOH<br>• Silent zero-clamping | Exactly 8 negative snapshots quarantined with \$ -672.45 inventory impact documented. | **Approved** | Data Quality Lead |
| **DEC-015** | 2026-10-29 | **Warehouse Technology** | Implement **DuckDB** as the embedded SQL analytical engine (`data/processed/inventory_analytics.duckdb`). | High-performance columnar SQL execution with zero external server dependencies, full ACID compliance, and native Pandas interoperability. | • SQLite<br>• PostgreSQL<br>• In-memory Pandas only | Enables millisecond-level window functions over 273,742 daily snapshot rows. | **Approved** | Analytics Engineer |
| **DEC-016** | 2026-10-29 | **Supplier Variance Buffer Decomposition** | Mathematically decompose safety stock into demand variance vs supplier delivery variance: $\Delta SS = SS_{\text{actual}} - SS_{\text{deterministic}}$. | Proves to procurement leadership the exact working capital (\$287.3K network total) required strictly due to vendor lead-time unreliability. | • Track on-time % only<br>• Ignore supplier variance | Enables data-driven vendor SLA negotiations and identifies high-risk suppliers (SUP-05, SUP-02). | **Approved** | Procurement Lead |
